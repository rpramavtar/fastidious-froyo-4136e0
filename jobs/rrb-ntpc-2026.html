Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is there a negative marking in RRB NTPC? +

Yes, there is a negative marking of 1/3rd mark for each incorrect answer in both CBT-1 and CBT-2.

Will I get an application fee refund if I do not appear for the exam? +

No, the fee refund is only applicable to candidates who actually appear for the Stage-1 CBT.
